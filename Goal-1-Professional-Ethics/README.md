## 1. Why I selected this learning goal

Before this learning plan, I had some experience discussing fairness, privacy and responsible technology use through IT studies and software projects, but limited knowledge of Indigenous communities, Indigenous data governance and culturally appropriate practices in digital systems. Understanding these responsibilities matters to software professionals, especially when designing systems that handle community data or use generative AI.

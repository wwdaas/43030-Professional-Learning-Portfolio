## Recommended A3 structure (maximum six pages)

1. **Introduction and professional pathway:** How the two chosen senior job advertisements and A1 Skills Audit informed priorities.
2. **Professional development activities and justification:** Discuss each of the three A1 goals and why the learning was relevant.
3. **Evidence of achievements and portfolio integration:** Show what was genuinely completed, with dates, evidence IDs and direct links.
4. **Critical reflection:** Compare earlier understanding with documented new practice; discuss challenges, surprises, limitations, and implications for responsible professional work.
5. **Future development goals:** Propose specific, measurable and time-bound next steps aligned to career goals.
6. **Conclusion and references (where appropriate):** Summarise supported achievements and cite any external frameworks or resources used.

## Evidence writing formula

**Planned activity (A1) → what I actually did → tangible outcome → evidence link → what I learned → remaining gap and next action.**

Good reflective writing must explain **how** thinking or behaviour changed. A description of tasks alone does not demonstrate critical reflection.

## Portfolio-link sentence template

"My original learning plan identified [specific skills gap]. I undertook [actual activity] and produced [verifiable outcome], documented as [E#.#](Evidence/README.md). This experience changed my understanding of [specific concept] because [reflection grounded in evidence]."

Replace each bracket only with verified facts.
